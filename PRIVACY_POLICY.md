## 1. Scope

This policy explains how Comprezza ("the app") handles information when you use
the Android application published by Dzynova Technologies ("we", "us"). The app
is designed as an offline-first photo compression and conversion tool. Selected
images — one at a time or several at once in a batch — are processed locally on
your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

## 2. Information the app accesses

Depending on the actions you take, the app may access:

- Images you explicitly select through Android's user-mediated photo picker,
  individually or as a batch.
- Images you capture with the device camera when you choose the camera option.
  The camera option opens your device's system camera app; Comprezza does not
  request camera permission. The photo is returned to the app, staged in
  app-private storage, and processed locally like any selected image. The system
  camera app is a separate application governed by its own privacy practices.
- Local image metadata needed to inspect dimensions, format, and file size.
- Compression preferences and local settings that you configure.
- Generated temporary outputs and images you export, stored on your device.
- Local history and statistics that you create through the app's own features.

This information is intended to remain on your device. Whether you process a
single image, a camera capture, or a batch of images, the app does not
intentionally upload selected images or generated outputs to Dzynova
Technologies or to any third-party cloud service.

## 3. Information the app does not collect

The current build is designed without accounts, advertising, behavioral
analytics, tracking, contact lists, location collection, or crash-reporting
services. The app does not sell personal information.

The release owner must verify these statements against the exact signed AAB and
all transitive dependencies before publication, and the Google Play Data Safety
form must be completed to match the final artifact.

## 4. Sharing and exports

**Sharing:** When you explicitly choose Android's share action, the app hands
the selected generated output to the Android system share sheet. The receiving
app may process, store, or transmit that file under its own privacy policy; we
do not control the receiving app and cannot be responsible for its practices.

**Saving:** When you choose Save to Device, the output is written to the
user-visible `Pictures/Comprezza` collection through Android's scoped MediaStore
APIs. The file remains on your device and is not transmitted anywhere by the app.

**External links:** The Settings and About screens provide links to the public
website and the app's Google Play listing. Opening those links leaves the app
and is subject to the destination service's own terms and privacy practices.

## 5. Storage and retention

Temporary generated files (including camera captures awaiting processing) are
stored in app-private storage and are reclaimed according to the app's cleanup
policy (including after failed operations and on a time-based cleanup schedule).
Exported files remain where you saved them until you or another device
application deletes them. Local history recorded from single, batch, or camera
flows is stored on-device. Clearing the app's cache, history, or data follows
the labels and confirmation dialogs shown in the app.

## 6. Your choices and controls

- You decide which images to select or capture, whether to process them
  individually or as a batch, and which to compress, save, and share.
- You can delete local history and clear generated cache files from the app.
- You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes app-private data.
- Files you exported to shared storage are managed by your device's normal
  storage controls.

## 7. Security

The app uses Android platform storage controls and validates managed file paths
for its local export workflow. No software can guarantee absolute security.
Keep your device and Android system updated, and review the recipient before
sharing sensitive images with other applications.

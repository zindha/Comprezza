# Comprezza 1.0.0 — Release Candidate Notes

## Highlights

- Offline photo compression with adjustable JPEG quality.
- Local before/after previews and storage-saving estimates.
- Android Photo Picker-compatible image selection.
- Scoped-storage-safe export to `Pictures/Comprezza`.
- Android Sharesheet sharing through managed temporary files.
- Material 3 light, dark, and system themes.
- English and Hindi localization foundations.
- Local settings, accessibility preferences, and privacy-safe configuration export.

## Privacy

Comprezza is designed to process selected images locally. No account, cloud
upload, analytics, behavioral tracking, or advertising service is included in
this build. Sharing is user-initiated through the Android system Sharesheet.

## Known limitations for RC1

- Advanced format conversion, resize, batch, folder, and history integrations
  remain staged behind their reviewed architecture boundaries.
- Public privacy-policy, legal, support, and store destinations require final
  product/legal approval before production submission.
- Android OEM, low-memory, accessibility, and performance validation must be
  completed on the release artifact.

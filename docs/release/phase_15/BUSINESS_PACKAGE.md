# Comprezza — Business and Ecosystem Package

**Status:** Strategy only; no payments, ads, subscriptions, or premium restrictions are implemented in the current release.

## Premium roadmap

### Foundation
- Premium capability and feature-gate contracts.
- Offline fail-closed behavior.
- No restriction of existing free workflow.

### Future candidates
- Unlimited batch processing.
- Folder compression.
- Advanced export/reporting.
- Advanced statistics and benchmark comparisons.
- Priority queue.
- Ad-free experience.

Every candidate requires a separate value, privacy, accessibility, battery, and Play review.

## Monetization strategy

Potential future models:

1. One-time Pro unlock for durable utility value.
2. Optional subscription only for genuinely recurring service costs, not for offline basics.
3. Optional rewarded action only if it never interrupts compression, export, sharing, or accessibility.

No model should degrade the core free workflow or create dark patterns.

## Pricing recommendations

Do not set a final price until market research, regional pricing, tax, refund, and support costs are known. Use a transparent regional one-time price for stable offline capabilities; reserve subscription for future ongoing services that users can understand.

Required review:

- [ ] Regional pricing research.
- [ ] Google Play Billing policy review.
- [ ] Restore-purchase and entitlement behavior.
- [ ] Offline grace/fail-closed behavior.
- [ ] Refund/support workflow.
- [ ] Accessibility and privacy review.

## Upgrade flow

1. Explain the capability and value in plain language.
2. Show price and billing period before confirmation.
3. Avoid blocking existing free workflows.
4. Use Google Play Billing only after a separate integration review.
5. Restore purchases clearly.
6. Handle cancellation, refund, offline, and account-change states honestly.

## Subscription philosophy

Subscriptions must represent recurring value. Do not place ordinary local compression behind a recurring payment merely to create pressure. No account or billing service is part of the current release.

## Ad strategy

Ads are not present in the current build. If ever introduced:

- Never interrupt compression, export, sharing, settings, history, error recovery, or accessibility announcements.
- Avoid ads adjacent to destructive actions.
- Do not use behavioral tracking without a separate consent/privacy decision.
- Review Families, consent, ad SDK, network, battery, and Data Safety implications.

## Future product ecosystem

- **Comprezza Video:** local video optimization; separate codec, battery, thermal, storage, and Play review.
- **Comprezza PDF:** local document/image workflows; separate file/privacy/accessibility review.
- **Comprezza Convert:** format conversion with explicit metadata policy.
- **Comprezza Resize:** predictable dimensions and batch workflows.

Shared settings should remain privacy-safe and format-specific where behavior differs. Cloud backup and AI require an explicit policy change and a new security review.

# Comprezza — Support and Marketing Package

## FAQ

### Does Comprezza upload my photos?
The current product is designed to process selected images locally. Verify the published privacy policy and exact release build before making a final claim.

### Do I need an account?
No account is required by the current documented scope.

### Where are saved images stored?
Save to Device uses Android's scoped MediaStore flow and targets `Pictures/Comprezza`.

### Can I share an image?
Yes. Choose Share to open Android's system share sheet. The receiving app controls what happens next.

### Can I compress several photos at once?
Yes. Choose Batch to select multiple images and process them in one queue with progress, retry, and cancel.

### Can I capture a photo with the camera and compress it?
Yes. The Compress workflow includes a camera action that sends the captured photo through the same local compression flow.

### Does compression reduce quality?
Compression may change visual quality, size, dimensions, or metadata. Review the output before replacing the original.

### Why is a format unavailable?
Codec support depends on the source image, Android device, and exact release scope.

### How do I report a bug?
Use the template below and do not attach private images unless support requests them and you have consent.

## Support workflow

1. Acknowledge and classify: crash, data loss, export/share, privacy, accessibility, performance, or general question.
2. Remove personal data from the ticket and request only necessary diagnostics.
3. Reproduce on the closest Android/OEM matrix.
4. Escalate security/privacy issues privately.
5. Record affected versions and severity.
6. Communicate workaround or fix without promising an unverified date.
7. Close only after confirmation or documented resolution.

# Comprezza — Play Store Screenshot Storyboard

**Important:** The requested concepts below are marketing storyboards. A screenshot may be produced only after the corresponding screen is fully implemented, populated with real data, accessibility-reviewed, and present in the exact release AAB. Do not create fictional screenshots for Play submission.

## Screenshot 1 — Immediate hook

- **Headline:** Make room for what matters.
- **Subheading:** Compress photos locally and keep control of your files.
- **UI:** Real Comprezza home/compression entry screen with one selected sample image or approved empty state.
- **Callouts:** Local workflow; clear select-image action; visible brand mark.
- **Purpose:** Communicate value in under two seconds.
- **Gate:** Must not show fake savings or an unreleased dashboard.

## Screenshot 2 — Smart compression

- **Headline:** Choose the quality that fits.
- **Subheading:** Balance image detail and file size with a simple control.
- **UI:** Real quality control, original preview, compressed preview, output size, and savings.
- **Callouts:** Quality slider; dimensions; file-size comparison.
- **Purpose:** Show control without technical complexity.
- **Gate:** Use only controls that affect the uploaded artifact's output.

## Screenshot 3 — Batch compression

- **Headline:** More photos, one clear workflow.
- **Subheading:** Batch compression is implemented in the current build; capture from the real release artifact after device validation.
- **UI:** Real batch picker/queue with actual progress and recovery.
- **Callouts:** Bounded queue; retry; cancellation.
- **Purpose:** Demonstrate scale and confidence.
- **Gate:** Closed once the batch flow passes device validation on a signed release artifact (picker, queue, retry/cancel, export).

## Screenshot 4 — Compare slider

- **Headline:** See the difference before you save.
- **Subheading:** Use only if an actual compare interaction exists in the release AAB.
- **UI:** Functional before/after comparison with meaningful image and size information.
- **Callouts:** Original; optimized; savings.
- **Purpose:** Make quality preservation tangible.
- **Gate:** Never depict a non-existent interactive gesture.

## Screenshot 5 — Offline privacy

- **Headline:** Your photos stay on your device.
- **Subheading:** Local processing, no account, no cloud upload in the current product design.
- **UI:** Real privacy/settings surface and local-processing indicator.
- **Callouts:** Offline processing; no account; user-controlled sharing.
- **Purpose:** Convert privacy intent into understandable proof.
- **Gate:** Publish only with a final HTTPS privacy policy and matching Data Safety answers.

## Screenshot 6 — Statistics dashboard

- **Headline:** Understand the space you save.
- **Subheading:** Local history and savings insights are implemented; capture with real device data or the approved empty state.
- **UI:** Actual history/statistics screen with non-fabricated user data or a clearly labeled empty state.
- **Callouts:** Saved storage; sessions; time period.
- **Purpose:** Encourage repeat use.
- **Gate:** Closed once the history/insights screen is validated on a signed release artifact with real or clearly labeled empty-state data.

## Screenshot 7 — Material You

- **Headline:** A workflow that feels at home.
- **Subheading:** Personalize theme, text, density, contrast, and motion.
- **UI:** Real Settings/Material 3 screen rendered in light/dark/system variants.
- **Callouts:** Theme; dynamic text; reduced motion; touch targets.
- **Purpose:** Demonstrate polish and accessibility.
- **Gate:** Validate on real devices and do not claim dynamic color unless the adapter is enabled.

## Screenshot 8 — Camera capture

- **Headline:** From shutter to smaller file.
- **Subheading:** Capture from the in-app camera and compress the result in the same local workflow.
- **UI:** Real camera capture entry in the compression workflow with the resulting compressed output.
- **Callouts:** Camera capture; local processing; savings.
- **Purpose:** Show a complete capture-to-compress loop.
- **Gate:** Capture only from the real workflow on a signed device; do not stage a camera scene.

## Production capture checklist

- [ ] Capture from signed release candidate, not a mockup.
- [ ] Remove personal data and test-only labels.
- [ ] Verify English listing copy and translated listing assets separately.
- [ ] Check contrast, text size, and legibility at thumbnail scale.
- [ ] Archive original captures and final composites.
